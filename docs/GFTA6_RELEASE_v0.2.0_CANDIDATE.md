# GFTA6 v0.2.0 — ONE MORE ERRAND™
## Draft release notes — NOT YET TAGGED

**THE INTERNET IS FURIOUS. YOU'RE JUST TRYING TO FIND PARKING.**

Suburbania now has an entire street full of opinionated, non-colliding
traffic. The original difficult Saturday survives alongside two
standalone aerial-combat modes.

### Included

- Original Classic 2D and Raised 2.5D city with extruded playable
  hatchback, coupe and van. Building depth sorting and shadow effects.
- THE ENGINE HAS HAD ENOUGH™ — deterministic, intentionally robotic
  F-UH-K vehicle motor linked to speed and throttle. The popular idle
  muttering is preserved byte-for-byte.
- SUBURBANIA HAS LOST ITS PATIENCE™ — six ambient cars, six walkers,
  distinct synthetic voicebanks, absurd spoken insults, captions and
  speech bubbles. **No NPC collisions**: this is an aesthetic decision.
- ONE MORE ERRAND™ — independent NPC-voice mute that preserves the
  engine and captions, viewport-safe bubbles, atmospheric minimap dots,
  and clearer mode-specific interface.
- Original Saturday errands, boot/courier economy, restoration and
  Freedom Flight, canonical endings, six-wave TOO CLOSE FOR GUNS,
  Advanced Air War with source-pinned VANILLAJS CAD, tactics,
  trajectory telemetry, retained LOD and sealed offline World Foundry.

### Engineering

Offline-first HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no runtime npm dependencies,
CDN or telemetry. Fixed-tick gameplay and replay rules are untouched;
audio and NPCs remain non-authoritative presentation layers.
Exact source-pinned vendor files and static offline cache are verified.

mode. MUSIC, SFX, and NPC VOICES are independently controlled.
